# Weekly

A simple, minimalist weekly planner web app. Open it on your phone and use Safari's **Share → Add to Home Screen** so it works like an app, offline too.

## Features
- **Day view**: one day at a time. Tap the day tabs or swipe left/right to move between days.
- **Week view**: all 7 days at a glance. **Month view**: tap any date to open it.
- **Stickers**: tap **+** for the sticker tray, then drag a sticker onto a day or tap it to add. Drag stickers to reorder them or move them to another day. Tap one to edit its label or time, or to delete it.
- **Wake time**: tap the WAKE line on any day to change it.
- **Every week vs. just this date**: by default, edits apply to the repeating weekly template. Switch to "Just M/D" to change only that date, and use "Use template" to undo the override.
- **Backup**: Export or Import your plan as a JSON file. **Start blank** clears everything, and **Load sample week** brings back the sample week.
- Your data stays on your device in `localStorage`.

Plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
